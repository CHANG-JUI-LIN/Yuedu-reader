1.1The river kept its counsel through the long dry season of the north,
and the herons waited on the stones as if the water owed them something.
1.2Then the rains came down from the hills in a single grey afternoon,
and every field that had been dust by morning was a mirror before dusk.
1.3The old men counted the days on their fingers and argued about the year,
while the children ran along the banks to see how far the flood had climbed.
