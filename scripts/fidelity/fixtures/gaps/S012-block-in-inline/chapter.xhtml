The phone buzzed twice on the kitchen table while the kettle was still working its way up to a boil.

Are you still coming to the market tomorrow?

Yes, but not before nine. The bus timetable changed again.

Then bring the big basket.

And an umbrella.

She read the messages twice and put the phone face down beside the cups.

Someone had pinned a card to the noticeboard by the door:
Lost, one grey cat, answers to Pepper, very fond of sardines.
It had been there since spring.

The kettle clicked off. Outside, the rain had started again, quiet and steady on the roof of the shed.
