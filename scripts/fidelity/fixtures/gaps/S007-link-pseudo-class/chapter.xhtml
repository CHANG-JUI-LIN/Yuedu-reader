The report was filed on a Tuesday, which turned out to matter later.[1] Nobody read it for a week, and when somebody finally did, the figures had already changed twice.[2]

Each of the bracketed numbers is a link, and each one is a little larger than the text around it, so the lines that hold them stand a little taller.[3] If the larger size is lost, the numbers shrink and the lines close up.

1. Filed in the morning, logged in the afternoon.

2. Once by the clerk and once by the author.

3. This is the last note.
