# 呈現方式 preview covers

`Resources/Assets/layout-sample-<slug>.jpg`, drawn by `HomeLayoutSample` (`Modules/Features/Explore/Home/HomeLayoutChooser.swift`).
Chosen 2026-10-13 at the maintainer's request: each layout its own covers, no book in two layouts,
not only Gutenberg's. `LayoutPreviewTests` holds the rules: no cover or title twice, and no
series or magazine twice (two issues of *Life* read as a repeat), anywhere.

Rule: every cover is in the public domain worldwide.

- Standard Ebooks dedicates its covers to the public domain (CC0). Each is built from a painting;
  the painting must also be clear, so each book's colophon was read and the painter checked
  (died before 1956).
- The rest are scans on Wikimedia Commons, some from the Rijksmuseum, the Met or the Cleveland
  Museum of Art (CC0), of a work whose artist died before 1956, or whose artist is unknown and
  which was published before 1931 (70 years from publication has long passed, and the US term
  too).

Cropped to 2:3 and saved at 300×450, quality 74 (74 files, about 2.3 MB).

2026-10-25, for the layouts taken from Apple Books (`Technotes/AppleBooksHomeLayouts-2026-10-25.md`),
26 more: 色帶書架, 大型封面書架 and 一大四小, and two lists each for 分類卡片 and 拼貼卡片.
Upload.wikimedia.org answered 429 for some originals, so files wider than 1920 px were taken
from Commons' 1920 px thumbnail, and PDF or DjVu pages from Commons' page render (猛虎集 at
960 px); at 300×450 nothing is lost.

2026-10-27: the colour band became the home's 頂部輪播圖 and left 呈現方式, so its four covers
went to 大圖卡片's two lists, 日本裝幀 and 新文學, with two more: 吶喊 and 紅燭. Left out for them:
墳's Commons image (a design sketch with notes), 烏合叢書's (a sketch, and the series 彷徨 is in),
野草 (孫福熙, died 1962), later 紅燭 reprints (pale or relabelled), and セノオ楽譜 and
コドモノクニ again (not books).

## Left out on purpose

- Standard Ebooks' *The Count of Monte Cristo*: its painting is Mead Schaeffer's, 1928; he died
  1980. *The Great Gatsby*: Francis Cugat, died 1981. Both are public domain in the US only.
- Puck, Christmas 1899 and Easter 1900: Frank A. Nankivell, died 1959.
- Racial or political caricature: Tokyo Puck December 1907, Happy Hooligan "An Uprising in
  China", Les Pieds Nickelés, Puck and Judge political covers, Lustige Blätter 1906 No. 40.
- A later reprint whose artist is unknown: Buster Brown Comic Book No. 35.
- A Foxy Grandpa image that is a detail of Louis M. Glackens' Puck cartoon; the other Foxy
  Grandpa scans are strips, not covers.
- A 苦悶的象徵 edition translated by 豐子愷 (died 1975).
- Second issues of one magazine or series (Life 1921, Good Housekeeping 1915 by the same Coles
  Phillips, Jugend Nr. 31, ホトトギス 1907 and 1908, Buster Brown 1908, *Tige: His Story*,
  which is Buster Brown's dog's book), and a second 夏目漱石 in one layout (鶉籠).
- Magazines and sheet music where the Eastern layouts want books: 青鞜, 明星, 宵待草 (a セノオ
  楽譜 score).

2026-10-25:

- 杉浦非水 (died 1965): ナカニシヤ's 日本一の画噺, 夢の華, the *Present-Day Japan* cover.
- CC-licensed scans rather than public-domain ones: *Lost in the Ice* (Pluck and Luck 160),
  Edmund Dulac's *Fairy-Book*, Hetzel's *Maître Antifer* and *Hatteras* photographs (CC BY-SA,
  CeCILL); かがみの国のアリス (a CC BY-SA photograph of a 1948 edition, and a second Alice).
- Magazines and sheet music, not books: セノオ楽譜, コドモノクニ, 婦人グラフ.
- A second 竹久夢二 or a series: 夢二画集 春／夏／秋／冬, 野に山に, 夢二抒情画選集 (two volumes);
  どんたく was too dark.
- ちりめん本 (the Rijksmuseum's Japanese Fairy Tale Series): clear, but pale and stamped by the
  library. 稻草人 (1923): dull, and its illustrator uncertain.
- Racial or religious caricature: Larry Lee (Pluck and Luck 886), Pluck and Luck 716 and 266,
  Secret Service 152, Frank Reade's "Dog-Faced Men", New Nick Carter Weekly 11, 辟邪紀實.
- A second of one series or author: *A Great Conspiracy* and New Nick Carter Weekly (Nick Carter),
  *The Lost World* and *The Hound of the Baskervilles* (Doyle), *Le Docteur Ox* (Voyages
  extraordinaires).
- Covers that do not crop to 2:3 or are not covers: *The Wonderful Wizard of Oz* (about 0.75,
  its title cut), *Tanglewood Tales* (a "Digitized by Microsoft" mark), *Le Mystère de la chambre
  jaune* (only an advert's detail), Merian's *Metamorphosis* (type alone), Kunstformen's plates.
- Plain cloth: *Tarzan of the Apes* (1914, its designer's dates unknown), *King Solomon's
  Mines*. *Fame and Fortune* (1921): too small.
