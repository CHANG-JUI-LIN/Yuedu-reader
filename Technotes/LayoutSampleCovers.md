# 呈現方式 preview covers

`Resources/Assets/layout-sample-<slug>.jpg`, drawn by `HomeLayoutSample` (`Modules/Features/Explore/Home/HomeLayoutChooser.swift`).
Chosen 2026-10-13 at the maintainer's request: each layout its own covers, no book in two layouts,
not only Gutenberg's.

Rule: every cover is in the public domain worldwide. Standard Ebooks dedicates its covers to the
public domain (CC0) and builds them from public-domain paintings. The rest are scans on Wikimedia
Commons of works whose artist died before 1956, or anonymous works published before 1900.
Cropped to 2:3 and saved at 300×450.

Left out on purpose: Standard Ebooks' *The Great Gatsby* (Francis Cugat, died 1981: public
domain in the US only); racial or political caricature covers (Tokyo Puck December 1907, Happy
Hooligan "An Uprising in China", Les Pieds Nickelés, Puck and Judge political covers); later
reprints whose artist is unknown (Buster Brown Comic Book No. 35); a Foxy Grandpa image that is a
detail of Louis M. Glackens' Puck cartoon; a 苦悶的象徵 edition translated by 豐子愷 (died 1975).
